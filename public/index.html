Hi, I'm Rachel

I'm an independent artist and illustrator living in my beloved home: the coastal forests and mountains of Oregon. My work grows out of our ecosystem and the plant allies in our community landscape.

Every block is carved by hand, every print pulled by hand, every shirt dyed by hand. No two are quite alike, and I think that's the best part.
